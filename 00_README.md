# cad-projects
Some of my CAD projects; the iterations of my rover, my drones, a pisa-based pencil holder, and my beach clean-up robot!

The files are sorted in order from most (01) to least complex (09), and they're the highlights of my high school career...
They are:
    01. My final rover design
    02. My rover design before final iterations (which was scrapped due to high cost, a primitive rocker-bogie iteration, the motors it was based around going out-of-stock, and an unnecessary convoluted assembly)
    03. My beach clean-up robot design (current, I have not iterated yet)
    04. My '26 ARC drone design
    05. My '25 ARC drone prototype (never used because the mission I was predicting DID NOT happen, but cool nonetheless)
    06. My first rover iteration (many problems with this, mostly too expensive, and when I found out it was too expensive, I didn't make the parts thick enough to actually support its own weight so this is the only "incomplete" design here)
    07. My somewhat scale-replica of the leaning tower of Pisa stylized into a pencil holder (the columns are larger to be more easily printable)
    08. My temporary prosthesis design for below-knee amputations
    09. My claw mechanism for the '25 year (again, was NOT the mission that ended up happening, but kind of cool regardless; this one is meshed weirdly for some reason so the sketches are weird?)

Yes, these are stylized like engineering drawings. They are not; no dimensions of course (but most of these builds have multiple complicated parts so this is the only way to view ALL the parts without being overwhelmed with other things)!
